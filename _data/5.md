The Ramathibodi Medical Student Council is thrilled to officially introduce the newly revamped RAMSC Central Portal, a unified digital home engineered to streamline every facet of student life across our pre-clinical and clinical years. Built to replace fragmented communication channels and outdated file drives, this next-generation platform consolidates academic resources, council updates, event registration, and administrative workflows into a single, intuitive interface. Starting today, students can seamlessly navigate integrated lecture note repositories, access vetted study guides contributed by senior peers, track real-time faculty announcements, and register for student-led extracurricular workshops without ever having to sift through disconnected group chats or misplaced cloud folders again.

Beyond functioning as an academic archive, the portal has been architected with powerful student engagement features designed to keep our community closely connected throughout rigorous schedules. Users can now easily submit club funding proposals, view interactive calendars outlining upcoming sports leagues, webinars, and volunteer initiatives, or submit direct, anonymous feedback regarding student welfare and clinical training conditions directly to the council. 

Optimized for both mobile devices and desktop workstations to accommodate students on the move between lecture halls and hospital rounds, this platform represents a major commitment to transparency, accessibility, and modernizing our student infrastructure. We invite all medical students to explore the new features, update their user profiles, and take full advantage of the centralized tools built to support your daily journey.
