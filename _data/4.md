Balancing a demanding medical curriculum with personal health is an ongoing challenge, which is why we are thrilled to announce the official launch of our new comprehensive Well-being Website. This dedicated digital platform has been built from the ground up to serve as your centralized hub for mental health resources, stress management tools, and peer-support networks.

Navigating medical school shouldn't mean sacrificing your own health. The new site features easy booking for counseling sessions, guided mindfulness routines, and directories for academic advising. We encourage everyone to bookmark the new site and take full advantage of the tools designed to help you thrive both academically and personally throughout the year.
