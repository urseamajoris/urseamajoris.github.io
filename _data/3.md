Get ready to bring your energy because the annual Syringe Games Sports Festival is just around the corner! It is time to step away from the textbooks, take a well-deserved break from clinical rotations, and show off our athletic prowess against fellow medical students. This festival is our biggest opportunity of the year to build camaraderie outside the classroom.

Whether you are smashing shuttles on the badminton court, hitting the track for a long run, joining the football tournament, or simply bringing the noise from the grandstands, we need everyone’s spirit to make this year's festival unforgettable. Captains will be finalizing team tryouts and practice schedules shortly, so keep an eye on your class group chats for sign-up sheets.
